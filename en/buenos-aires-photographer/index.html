01 / Make the memory yours

Photoshoots for couples, families & solo travelers

A vacation photoshoot gives everyone a place in the picture. Share a walk as a couple, bring the family or make time for yourself during a solo trip. Your personal photographer helps shape the session around your group and the kind of photographs you would like to take home.

Couples

A shared walk, small gestures and portraits together. Plan a couple photoshoot with room for movement and quiet moments, without having to pose for every picture.

Families

Tell us who is coming and the ages of any children. We can plan a manageable route and discuss breaks so the session fits the people taking part.

Solo travelers

Step out from behind your phone and into your travel photographs.

02 / Time to be yourself

Personal portraits, with Buenos Aires around you

A private photoshoot can capture your style, a new chapter or simply the feeling of being somewhere new. Share references and bring clothes that feel like you. We plan the setting together and offer guidance with posture, hands and movement, so you do not need previous experience in front of a camera.

03 / Find your backdrop

Locations for your Buenos Aires photoshoot

The best location for you depends on the light, the look you like and the time you have. These neighborhoods are starting points for planning, rather than a fixed itinerary. Meeting points, access, permissions and any location fees are confirmed before booking.

Palermo

Consider a route with greenery and open spaces. We agree on a specific meeting point and keep travel between spots practical for the session.

Recoleta

An option for an urban setting with architecture and plazas. The route depends on timing, light and how busy the area is.

Puerto Madero

For a waterfront setting and contemporary lines. Distances and weather help determine the route and timing.

FRAME / FAQ

Before your photoshoot

Do I need to know how to pose?

No. You can share reference photographs, but we offer guidance during the session to help you find comfortable postures and movements.

What should I wear?

Choose comfortable clothes that suit the location and feel like you. If you want outfit changes, we agree in advance on the time needed and where changing is possible.

What happens if it rains?

We agree on rescheduling conditions or an alternative before booking. If your trip has limited dates, mention this in your first message so we can take it into account.

Can I choose the location?

Yes. Suggest a neighborhood or share references, and we will review the route, access and timing together.

How many photos will I receive, and when?

The number of edited photographs and delivery date are confirmed in your proposal before booking. They depend on the session you agree on.
